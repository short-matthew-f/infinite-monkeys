# Pop-up Floor Plan, round 3

**Idea.** Same pop-up floor as round 2 (an office floor on a table, cutouts standing on it, rims and two shadow tiers), polished toward the style reference: the first screen now shows all four places, every tappable place carries a cue that reads in a still frame, the UI is cut paper too, the hire is a walk, and the page spends less when things are off screen.

**First screen.** Resting zoom is 0.8 (was 1.0). Zones were pulled into a cluster: Departments and the Director (each scaled to 0.88) sit either side of the water-cooler nook, the Typing Pool is 45 px higher and 20 px shorter, and Personnel is tucked 75 px under the Pool with its desk and monkey fully in frame. Departments and the Director are cut by the screen edges (about 70% visible, sign and nearest cabinet or glass wall), Personnel's pad is cut at the bottom, and aisle props (plants, shredder, mail cart) peek in at the sides. The floor is 780 x 1250 world px (about 1.6 screens each way) and still pans with momentum, now at the resting zoom. The Personnel hot area is on screen in the default view, so it can be tapped before Ernest arrives.

**Tap cues that read without motion.** Each tappable pad sits on a visible paper edge with a two-tier cast shadow and a big lifted dog-ear. Each has a sign that clearly hangs: a brass rail on two posts, cords and eyelets, a plate that swings a little. Each has fully lit floor lamps (shade, bulb, rays, pool of light); only a faint outer ring breathes, and only at Personnel. Decor never gets a sign, lamp or lifted pad. While nothing has been hired, a cut-paper pointing hand bobs above Personnel's sign; it goes away after the first hire.

**Vacant desks.** Blotter, a sheet in the platen, pen stand, an empty olive chair with a tangerine cushion and tufting, and a mustard VACANT tent card. A seated monkey replaces all of it.

**Paper UI.** Directory and room sheets are cream cards with a die-cut cream rim, a second sheet peeking out behind, a ruled inner border, a two-tier shadow and a notched folder tab. Numbers are brass discs (rooms 1 to 4; Floor 2 is a dashed "F2" plate under an "Above" heading, so no number repeats). Headers are typed (Courier Prime form lines, double rule). Contents are printed forms: ruled fields with dot leaders, desk slots drawn as tiny desks, brass-bezel dials with ticks and colour bands, ledger bars with hatching and a brass end cap, a double-ruled rubber stamp, and button faces with a lifted lip. A sheet is capped at 40dvh with a fixed header and a scrolling body; Hire is above the fold, Buy desk scrolls. The camera frames the room in the space above (Personnel includes the entrance strip). The Directory opens below the header, so Bananas, Income and Monkeys stay visible.

**Ernest.** Peeks up over his card in the bottom-right aisle and points at Personnel (Personnel stays clear). Deliberate swap: the round 2 dotted paper arrow is gone; the bobbing hand over Personnel's sign does that job and his pencil only gestures toward it. `state.tut` is true from load, so the hand and the Hire nudge show even with `?noernest`, until the first hire. With a sheet open he docks as a narrow strip at the top right and the camera frames the room beside him. Add `?noernest` to skip him.

**Hiring.** Hire starts a tracking move (camera pulls back over the whole path), the waiting candidate walks entrance, left aisle, pool desk at 250 px/s (linear, with a stepped 2.5 px bob, facing flips), and sits (brief squash, brass glow); the next candidate comes through the door. Reduced motion: no travel, no pull-back animation (camera snaps), the monkey appears seated with a 1.7 s glow. If all drawn desks are taken the walker leaves toward the Pool aisle (extra bought desks have no seat drawn yet).

**Aisle dressing.** Eight identical ferns became three plants (ficus, snake plant, cactus, 2 to 3 each) plus an umbrella stand, a step ladder, a waste bin, a cork notice board, and a runner rug. The mail cart now loops the aisles at 42 px/s (route x 110 to 670, y 394 to 872).

**Faces and figures.** Editor: flat mouth and open eyes looking down at the paper (was frown and heavy lids). Director: glass is two narrow side panes at 12% tint with the silhouette standing clear, wall lightened. Figure value contrast: every fur is at least 3.17:1 against the surface it stands on in both themes (light darkens fur 42%, dark lifts it 50% toward the glow; measured from computed fills). White shirts on the light pads are still only 1.4:1 and rely on the rim and the dark fur of head and hands.

**Stretch.** It was a phase, not a bug: the arms were up for about 40% of the cycle. Now a 9 s cycle with arms up about 12% of the time (sampled: 3 of 24 s). Reduced motion holds a mid-stretch at +/-130 degrees.

**Performance.** Ten `.area` groups; the camera rectangle (in world coordinates, so it works mid-pan and from the target of an eased move) toggles `.offscreen`, which pauses every animation inside; `visibilitychange` pauses everything; Ernest's loop pauses while he is away. Default view: 65 running CSS animations (round 2: 81), 62 to 65 across the four panned corners (the Pool is always in view), 0 when hidden. DOM: 1,782 nodes at the default view, 1,821 with a sheet open (round 2: 1,559). Fixed along the way: the typewriter paper was styled as a sheet panel and invisible in round 2; it is now `.tsheet` and shows.

**Contrast.** All new pairs computed (brass disc and tab numerals 7.8 to 9.3:1, muted type on sheet and forms 5.3 to 6.0:1, stamp 5.2 to 6.8:1, button faces 6.6 to 12.8:1, focus ink 11 to 14:1 on sheet and floor). New token `--btn` (sheet mixed with edge) keeps button faces readable in dark. Computed, not measured on a device.

**Next.** Real sheet contents; the second floor stacked above; pinch zoom; a drawn seat for desks bought beyond ten; pre-render the static floor if the node count grows.
