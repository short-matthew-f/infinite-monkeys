Opus summary · Round 2

Where round 2 landed

  1. Much better, and most of the round 1 problems are fixed. Roominess went from 3 to 4 and paper depth from 3 to 4, and every checklist item now passes. The monkeys finally read as individuals.
  2. The weak spot is the first screen. Only the Typing Pool is fully in view, so a new player sees one tappable place, not four. This is the top fix for round 3.
  3. The paper look stops at the forms. The floor is paper, but the directory and room forms are flat app panels. They need the same die-cut, tab and shadow treatment.
  4. Small finishes: real vacant desks instead of grey blobs, fewer repeated ferns, a friendlier Editor, open forms capped at about 40% of the screen.

Next

Round 3 fixes the first screen, the tap cues, and the paper forms. After that, the panel’s view is that it’s ready to become the style reference for building the real game: the floor plan replaces the tab bar, and each existing screen becomes a room’s form.

Evidence: scripted Chrome screenshots at 390 × 844 (light, dark, reduced motion, animation frames, taps), computed contrast, and static inspection. Not yet tested on a physical phone or by players. Reviewers: a Sonnet agent for taste and a Haiku agent for the checklist on each approach.
